## Continued Guided Workday - React, Figma to React and Gesture Preparation

### Purpose of The Day

This workday is a continuation of the material from `26-05-2026`.

Use the day to keep working on the task you started yesterday, choose a new React exercise, continue your Figma-to-React prototype or prepare for the upcoming touch gesture lesson on `29-05-2026`.

I will be available for help, feedback and guidance. You can also ask me to help you choose an exercise that fits your current level.

By the end of the workday, you should have made concrete progress on your own work and know what your next step is.

---

### What To Do

Choose one of these options:

1. **Continue from yesterday**
   - Keep working on the same exercise, prototype or problem from `26-05-2026`.
   - Use the [Optional Guided Workday - Gesture & Motion Preparation](26-05-2026-guided-workday-gesture-motion.md) page if you need the exercise list.
2. **Choose a React exercise**
   - Pick an exercise that matches your level.
   - Ask for help if you are unsure which one to choose.
3. **Continue your Figma-to-React prototype**
   - Refactor components, improve layout, add interaction or clean up generated code.
4. **Prepare for Touch Gestures as Interface**
   - Try a small Motion experiment with `motion.div`, `whileTap`, `whileHover` or `drag`.
   - Think about where a touch, pointer, drag or swipe interaction could improve your own prototype.
