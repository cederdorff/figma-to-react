## Workday - Figma to React

### Purpose of The Day

This workday is for consolidating what you worked on in the `20-05-2026` Figma to React with MCP lesson and catching up on anything you need before the next class.

Use the time to continue your Figma-to-React prototype, strengthen your React fundamentals, or explore the MCP workflow further on your own.

---

### What To Do

Work on what makes most sense for you right now. Here are some good options:

1. **Continue the Figma to React with MCP workshop**
   - Use the Figma MCP Starter Guide to keep building and refining your prototype from yesterday.
   - Refactor generated output, add state and interaction, and make it feel less like a static screen.
2. **Keep improving the Codeagram app**
   - Continue working with components, props, state, events and code structure.
3. **Continue the CodeDex React course**
   - [CodeDex React course](https://www.codedex.io/react)
4. **Prepare for LYDA - Gesture & Motion Design (`22-05-2026`)**
   - Skim Pine & Gilmore's experience domains: [The Experience Economy](https://uen.pressbooks.pub/servicesmgt/chapter/chapter-17-the-experience-economy/)
   - Read how to build a Pepper's Ghost: [DIY Pepper's Ghost](https://makezine.com/projects/diy-hacks-how-tos-the-peppers-ghost-illusion/) og se videoen på siden.
   - **Bring a bottle, jar, lantern or glass container** to class (nothing bigger than A4).
   - Have a small idea for a theme to show in your Pepper's Ghost before arriving — e.g. _memory_ or _childhood_. Research what program or AI can help you create the digital content for it.
