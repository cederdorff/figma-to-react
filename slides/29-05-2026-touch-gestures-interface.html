What is Motion?

  • Motion is a React animation and interaction library.
  • It helps you make state changes visible and understandable.
  • It supports gestures such as drag, swipe, tap and hover.

Why learn it? Better feedback, clearer interaction flow, more expressive prototypes.

What is Haptic Feedback?

  • Haptics are tactile signals (small vibration feedback).
  • They can confirm success, warning, or error states.
  • Good haptics support meaning, not just effect.

Why learn it? It adds a second feedback channel alongside visuals and motion.
